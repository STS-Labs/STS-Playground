An Ode to Nikusha
by Zuka

Of all the files inside this repo,
of every branch and every line,
there's one thing that I'll always know:
Nikusha, friend, you're one of mine.

You write the code, I break the build,
you fix the bugs I leave behind,
with every merge my heart is filled —
a better pal I'll never find.

Though your .txt was not my style
(I'll say it plainly, no pretend),
it's worth the diff, it's worth the while,
to have you as my closest friend.

So here's my commit, signed and true,
pushed up to GitHub for all to see:
no branch could hold the love for you
that Zuka has, Nikusha — from me.
